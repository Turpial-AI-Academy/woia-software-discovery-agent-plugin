## 10. Discovery gate

Confirm before proceeding:

- [ ] The problem or opportunity is understandable without assuming a technical solution.
- [ ] Affected users / actors are identifiable.
- [ ] The expected outcome is distinct from implementation details.
- [ ] Immediate scope and exclusions are clear enough for the next step.
- [ ] Material inferences and unknowns are labeled.
- [ ] No downstream business-rule, requirement, UX, stack, architecture, or implementation decision is being presented as Discovery fact.
